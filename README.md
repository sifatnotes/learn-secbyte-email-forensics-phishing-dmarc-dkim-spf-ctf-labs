## Learning Path

1. **Start with email evidence:** Explore inbox investigation and email header analysis.
2. **Investigate suspicious content:** Practice reviewing URLs, attachments, and QR-code phishing scenarios.
3. **Identify indicators:** Explore email IOC hunting and phishing investigation.
4. **Understand authentication:** Study SPF, DKIM, and DMARC concepts and their relationship to email spoofing.
5. **Connect the evidence:** Use the investigation labs to build a structured approach to identifying and documenting suspicious email activity.

Follow any prerequisites or recommended sequence listed by the lab provider.

## How to Use the Labs

1. Open a lab using its direct link.
2. Review its learning objectives and environment requirements.
3. Complete the exercise in the authorized lab environment.
4. Document relevant evidence, indicators, and observations.
5. Summarize findings and consider suitable defensive controls.
